### Decisions already made (and why), so they are not re-litigated

- **The compute-tier overclaim is closed.** An 8B full fine-tune does not fit in the 128 GB unified
  pool; the row was corrected on 2026-10-14 and `conventions.md` now carries the rule that a hardware
  claim must survive the book's own arithmetic.
- **The judge is a diagnostic, not a reward.** Measured on the calibrated band, the 8B judge disagreed
  with the rule critic on 106 turns - about three quarters of the judged turns - systematically, over
  exactly the turns where the policy reads before acting. Fusing it into the reward would teach a
  policy not to look before it acts.
- **A served policy is not trained inside the loop.** `Config.served` drops the lineage and entropy
  checks rather than letting them fail: a frozen policy has no update to apply and no distribution to
  measure. The repertoire (distinct-action ratio) is reported in their place.

### Open work, in the order that pays first

1. **Chapter 13 (paper deep-dives)** is a stub and is the natural home for the results this project
   just produced: the flat-group arithmetic, calibration, and judge robustness.
2. **Chapters 8-12** are skeletons with outlines only.
3. **The RL update exists in-process** (step A-C, with a held-out check) but not on the *served* policy:
   pushing updated weights to an inference server between rounds is the weight-sync problem, and it
   belongs to Project 3 and Chapter 9.
4. **Projects 3-6** are README only.
5. **The elective modules** have prose but no figure.
